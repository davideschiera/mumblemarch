## Visual observations

- **Screen geometry (320×200 gameplay screen, Amiga and DOS alike):** the playfield is rows 0–159 (**160 px, 80 %**). The status text line is rows ~160–175 (16 px) and the skill-button row is rows 176–199 (**24 px**). The whole bottom panel is therefore **40 px, 20 % of the screen height**, and the playfield-to-panel ratio is 4:1. There are 12 buttons, each **16 px wide × 24 px tall** (192 px total, starting at x=0): RR−, RR+, climber, floater, bomber, blocker, builder, basher, miner, digger, pause, nuke. The minimap fills the rest to the right (about 104×22 px inside its frame), with a decorative mark on each side.
- **Sprite proportions:** a lemming is **about 10 px tall** (measured on 02: green hair 2 rows, pink-white face and hands, blue body, feet), roughly 4–6 px wide when walking and about 10 px wide as a blocker with arms out. That is **1/16 of the playfield height** and 1/32 of the screen width. Sprites are tiny compared with the terrain; crowds read as a band of green-topped blue ticks. In 02 the stair steps are brown/orange bands a couple of pixels thick with a darker shadow row beneath (exact brick size not measured).
- **Palette:** the Amiga background is dark navy **#000031** (RGB 0,0,49). DOS VGA uses pure black **#000000**. Lemming colours on Amiga: hair green (0,186,0), skin/hands pinkish-white (255,223,222), body blue (66,69,239). DOS is nearly the same: (0,176,0), (240,208,208), (64,64,224). Terrain tilesets are saturated and heavily dithered or noise-textured (dirt orange/brown, gold marble, pink brick, crystal blue). Each level has a small palette of about 16 colours. On DOS, the panel's button texture and minimap side marks take on level-palette colours (yellow-gray, brown, magenta and so on). On Amiga the panel is consistently brown with red marks.
- **Contrast:** there is high contrast between the dark background and bright terrain. Lemmings stay readable because of the green-hair / blue-body pairing against both.
- **Fonts:** the status line uses a large **green** font about 16 px tall with a shaded or dithered bevel look (`OUT 23  IN 00%  TIME 4-00`). Time is written `M-SS` with a hyphen. Amiga pads the percentage to `00%`; DOS shows `0%`. Skill counts are tiny 2-digit dark-on-white numerals in a box at the top of each button (blank box = 0). Preview and results text is a chunky pixel font with a dark outline. **Amiga colours each line differently** (red, blue, green, orange, cyan, magenta) over a dark-green mottled texture. **DOS uses a single blue-violet** over a dark red-brown rock texture.
- **Cursors:** in the playfield the cursor is a small **green dotted crosshair** with a bright centre. Over a lemming it becomes a **square bracket box**: Amiga has green corners with yellow midpoints, DOS has dashed segments in green, pink and blue. The status line then shows the lemming's skill and the count under the cursor (`WALKER 6`). In menus the Amiga shows a pink **hand pointer** with a blue cuff, and while busy it shows a **sleeping lemming in bed with "zzz"**.
- **Minimap:** it sits in the bottom-right of the panel. On Amiga it is a green monochrome terrain silhouette on black with a green viewport rectangle and a brown frame. On DOS it has a red frame, terrain in level colours or white, and a white or yellow viewport rectangle. Small yellow pixels (RGB 239,207,0) in 02's minimap are probably lemming dots.
- **Selected skill:** the chosen button gets a highlight frame (yellow on Amiga, white on DOS).
